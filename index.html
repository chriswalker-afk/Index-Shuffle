How to survive

Each valid slide spawns a new tile. From move 40, every tenth slide can add a second tile when space is available.

The first easy blocker appears at move 10 and needs just one adjacent merge. Another is attempted every 10 successful slides: the strength rises to 2, 3, 4 and then 5 merges (the maximum). At most two blockers can be active at once, and a blocker needs an empty square to appear.

Blockers stay fixed and divide rows and columns. Merges must finish directly beside them—not diagonally—to deal damage. Breaking a blocker opens its square for future turns.

New bases unlock at moves 15, 35 and 60. You lose when no legal slides or banking actions remain.
