How to survive

Each valid slide spawns a new tile. From move 40, every tenth slide can add a second tile when space is available.

A fixed blocker may appear from move 55, no more often than once every 30 moves and with only one active at a time. Blockers never move or merge: make three merges landing directly beside one (not diagonally) to break it and free its square. A newly cleared square remains empty for that move.

New bases unlock at moves 15, 35 and 60. You lose when no legal slides or banking actions remain.
