REFERENCE

How to play

1. Slide and combine

Use the arrow keys, WASD, touch swipe or direction buttons. Powers with the same base merge by adding their exponents. A swipe that changes nothing does not cost a move.

3² × 3³ = 3⁵

2. Bank a power

Select a glowing ✦ tile and calculate its exact value in 60 seconds. Every power can normally be banked only once per base–exponent combination each round, including golden powers. The rare Echo Seal allows one exception on its marked square. In Roguelike, gold allows banking exponent 1 directly, provided that exact power has not already been banked. Banking earns points and leaves a non-golden smaller tile behind. A wrong answer, timeout or cancelled question locks the tile until another successful slide; cancelling never resets the answer timer for free.

3. Protect your moves

You start with 10 moves. Each successful slide costs 1 move plus 1 for every active blocker. Bank two powers that were actually created by merging to earn back 1 move, up to 10. At zero moves the run ends.

4. Break blockers

Yellow ! squares warn where a fixed blocker will arrive on the next slide. A blocker cannot move or merge. Correctly bank a ⚡ merged power directly beside a blocker to damage it. Different blocker tiers take different numbers of successful banks.

Roguelike: earn points and coins

Each correctly banked power scores points equal to its exponent: 7² scores 2 points. Reach your round target before running out of moves. Clear twelve rounds to win the run.

Golden tiles have a full metallic appearance and a ◉ coin mark. You can bank any golden tile by calculating its value correctly: 3¹ = 3 is a valid bank and earns 1 coin. Or merge it first for a bigger payout: 2 coins for most merged golden powers, 3 for exponent 4 or higher, or 4 for double gold. Gold survives slides and merges; banking consumes the gold. A golden copy of an already-banked power cannot be banked again until the next round. Merge it into a different exponent first.

When you clear a round you also earn 1 coin per unused move (up to 5 bonus coins). Your coins carry forward to a shop after each cleared round. The shop has two single-use offers and two permanent offers, with two inventory slots for each type. Purchased items are saved and their effects are active. Tap a single-use item during a round, then choose the highlighted square(s), or cancel without consuming it. Rare Runestones create positive effects on a selected board square, lasting only this round. At most two may be active at once. The Gilded Foundry creates gold on a merge or correct bank; Power Prism boosts points up to a total ×4 multiplier; Echo Seal permits one correct repeat bank; Twin Mint turns one merged power double gold; Time Sanctuary grants 10 extra seconds on opening a question (up to 60 seconds); and Shatter Crest adds one blocker hit when a genuinely merged power is correctly banked beside a blocker. The first two, Sanctuary and Shatter have two charges; Echo and Twin Mint have one. Incorrect answers never award points, coins or blocker damage. Permanent bonuses apply automatically. Each defeated boss reduces your banking-answer time by 10 seconds for subsequent rounds: 60, then 50, then 40, then 30 seconds per question. Every third round brings a different boss rule, revealed in the preceding shop. Correctly bank powers to counter boss obstacles and survive the round. Against The Frostlock, one direction is frozen at a time: its on-screen arrow is disabled and that keyboard direction or swipe cannot move. Frozen attempts cost no moves; the ice switches after each successful slide, correct bank or item use.

From Stage 2 onward, up to two marked board squares may carry hazards. Cloudy hides a tile and prevents banking while it is covered; Void lowers an incoming exponent; Null blocks merging; Cracked becomes Null after a merge; and Greedy strips gold along a tile's path. A hazard warning appears one successful slide before a hazard activates. Buy a one-use Hazard Eraser in the shop to remove any active hazard without using a move, including a Cloudy square hiding a tile. Hazard Eraser can also rescue a board that has no other legal actions.

Permanent hazard defences appear in shops before the relevant hazards are unlocked. Some reduce hazard warnings or make a particular type less likely; Hazard Tamer changes incoming Null to Cracked and Cracked to Void. Others shorten Cloudy, prevent Void reducing a square to exponent 1, or protect gold passing through Greedy. Each defence is partial: hazards still matter. They occupy the same two permanent slots as scoring bonuses, so choose which advantages matter most.

INDEX LAW

am × an = am+n

Keep the base. Add the exponents.

This rule applies when the bases are identical. For example, 2² multiplied by 2³ gives 2⁵, not 2⁶.

Evaluate a power to bank it

The exponent tells you how many copies of the base to multiply.

3⁴ = 3 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 81

Banking asks for the number value, not another power expression. A golden 3¹ is simply 3. You have 60 seconds for each answer.

Watch out for this mistake

Multiplying two powers is not the same as adding their numerical values. The exponent rule gives:

2² × 2³ = 2⁵ = 32

Buying, replacing & selling

You have two permanent and two single-use inventory slots. When both slots of a type are full, buying offers a choice of what to replace. Selling a permanent returns half the amount you paid, rounded down. Discarded single-use items give no refund. Rerolls cost coins and get more expensive each time you reroll during this shop visit.

✦ Runestone field guide — effects, charges & unlocks

Rare one-use items enchant a chosen board square for the current round. They do not score anything when placed. Position matters: most effects trigger only on a merge or correct bank there. Each shop visit reveals at most one rare Runestone, even with rerolls. At most two enchanted squares can be active on the board.
