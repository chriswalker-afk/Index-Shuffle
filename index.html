How to survive

Start with 10 moves. Each successful slide costs 1 move, plus 1 for each active blocker already on the board: with one blocker, a slide costs 2 moves; with two, 3 moves. An incoming warning does not count until its blocker appears.

Every two merges return one move, including merges from different slides. Earned moves are credited before checking for defeat. If moves left reaches zero, the game ends—even if you could still bank a tile. Swipes that change nothing are free.

Each valid slide spawns a new tile. From move 40, every tenth slide can add a second tile when space is available.

At move 9, an empty square is highlighted as a warning. On your next successful slide (move 10), that exact square becomes a tier 1 blocker, requiring just one adjacent merge to destroy. The marked square is temporarily reserved, so tiles cannot slide through it or overwrite it.

A new warning is attempted every ten moves (19, 29, 39, …), leading to blockers with 2, 3, 4 and then 5 hits (the maximum). At most two blockers can be active at once. If no safe square is available, that warning and blocker are skipped.

Blockers stay fixed and divide rows and columns. Merging alone does not damage a blocker. Make a power by merging matching bases, then bank that merged power correctly while it is directly beside a blocker to deal one hit. Diagonal squares do not count; a power spawned randomly must first be merged before it can damage anything. Look for the ⚡ symbol on powers that can damage a blocker when banked.

Breaking a blocker opens its square for future turns. Incorrect or timed-out banking attempts never damage blockers.

New bases unlock after 15, 35 and 60 successful slides. You also lose if no legal slides or banking actions remain.
