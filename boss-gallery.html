The Curious Numberworld · Eight boss encounters

Meet your mathematical nightmares.

Eight possible opponents, four encounters per Roguelike run. Each portrait has its own silhouette and colour story. The game always explains the boss's actual effect; no one needs to guess the rules from the artwork. Select Preview encounter to inspect the in-game introduction without starting a game or altering any saved progress.

Every preview here is cosmetic. The actual bosses appear on rounds 3, 6, 9 and 12 and are revealed in the previous shop. Boss introductions can be skipped immediately and never change moves or scoring.
